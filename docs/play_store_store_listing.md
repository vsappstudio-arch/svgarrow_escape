## App title

```
ARROWW
```

Play Console allows up to 30 characters for the title. "ARROWW" (6
characters) is the app's actual branding everywhere in the app itself —
launcher icon, splash, About screen — so using it unchanged as the store
title keeps the Play listing consistent with what a player sees on-device.
There is room to add a short descriptive suffix if you want the listing
title itself to signal "puzzle game" at a glance, e.g. `ARROWW - Arrow
Puzzle Game` (24 characters) — optional, not required, and not something
this document invents a claim to justify (it's just genre-labeling, same
as the category field already does).

## Short description (max 80 characters)

```
A growing collection of challenging arrow puzzles. No ads, no accounts.
```

73 characters. Fits the 80-character limit with room to spare. Uses
"growing collection" rather than a fixed level count, so this copy stays
accurate as levels are added in future updates — see
`docs/play_store_release_checklist.md` for the current exact level count.

## Full description

```
ARROWW is a calm, focused puzzle game about clearing a grid of arrows in
the right order — tap an arrow and it flies off the board in the
direction it points, but only once nothing blocks its path.

• A growing collection of hand-designed levels, climbing from simple 3×3
  boards to intricate 12×12 challenges.
• Star ratings based on how few moves you use — chase 3 stars on every
  level, or just enjoy clearing the board.
• Hint and Undo when you're stuck, and an Extra Moves booster for when a
  level is genuinely tough.
• Track your progress — levels completed, total stars, and your best
  move count on every level, all saved on your device.
• No account needed. No ads. No in-app purchases. Play offline, anytime.

Optional reminders (Tuesday and Saturday) can nudge you back to your next
puzzle — entirely optional, and you're in control in Settings.
```
